Approaching Entropy Limits with Learned Lossless Compression

research_proposal

With increasingly more data being stored globally, significant economic and monetary costs come out of storage. Data centers are not small, cheap projects. They are large complexes that pollute the environment and require tons of resources that can become expensive. With this motivation, this project regarding data compression was conducted in order to answer the research question of whether or not entropy could be reached by compressors. Using learned compression, this paper was able to prove the improvement in text compression derived from using large language models. These greater results in compression may have the future impact of decreasing how much storage must be used to hold information digitally.
